About Us﻿

We are a not-for-profit cooperative association based in Stockholm. Our ca 60 members are individuals, worldwide: they are leading practitioners of education, coaching, facilitation and leadership in the area of sustainable/regenerative development and transformative learning.
We are partnering with nine other organisations, co-creating a new kind of online educational and community platform.
