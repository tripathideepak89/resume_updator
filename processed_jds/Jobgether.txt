About the job
This position is posted by Jobgether on behalf of a partner company. We are currently looking for a Platform Engineer - Multicloud in Sweden.

This is a senior infrastructure role focused on building and scaling cloud-agnostic platform systems that power large-scale developer-facing services. You will play a key role in designing and evolving infrastructure that can operate seamlessly across multiple cloud providers and potentially colocated environments. The work is highly technical and centered on Kubernetes, distributed systems, and infrastructure abstraction layers. You will collaborate closely with engineering and product teams to ensure that new systems are designed with portability and scalability in mind from the outset. This is a fast-moving, async-first environment where engineering rigor, autonomy, and systems thinking are essential. Your contributions will directly shape the foundation of a globally used developer platform serving millions of users.

Accountabilities

Audit existing infrastructure to identify cloud-specific dependencies and define a structured migration and portability roadmap
Design and implement cloud-agnostic abstractions across compute, networking, and storage layers to enable multi-cloud execution
Maintain and evolve Kubernetes-based orchestration systems to ensure reliable workload portability across environments
Collaborate with product and platform engineers to embed multi-cloud design principles into new system development
Contribute to architectural decisions regarding cloud strategy, infrastructure evolution, and colocated deployment options
Support ongoing improvements to platform scalability, resilience, and operational efficiency across distributed systems

Requirements

8+ years of experience in platform engineering, infrastructure engineering, or distributed systems roles
Strong hands-on experience operating Kubernetes in production across multiple cloud environments (AWS, GCP, Azure)
Proven experience with multi-cloud, cloud abstraction, or infrastructure re-platforming initiatives
Proficiency with infrastructure-as-code tools such as Pulumi or similar technologies
Strong systems design skills with deep understanding of compute, networking, and storage layers
Experience working in async, distributed, or globally remote engineering teams
Ability to communicate complex technical concepts clearly across technical and non-technical stakeholders
Strong ownership mindset with comfort operating in ambiguous, fast-paced environments
Nice to have: experience contributing to or building developer platforms at scale

Benefits

Fully remote work with global flexibility and asynchronous collaboration
Equity ownership (ESOP) allowing participation in company growth and success
Annual tech allowance to set up your ideal work environment
Comprehensive health coverage, including full employee coverage and partial dependent coverage
Annual global off-site gathering for collaboration, alignment, and team connection
Flexible working hours with high trust and autonomy over your schedule
Annual professional development budget for courses, books, and conferences
Co-working allowance or membership (e.g., WeWork access) for optional in-person work environments
