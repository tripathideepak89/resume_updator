Internship Description

This is an Integration and Automation Officer job. The officer will be responsible for designing, implementing, and managing automated workflows. Daily tasks include creating, configuring, and optimizing automation processes, integrating various systems/tools, troubleshooting errors, and maintaining smooth operations. The individual will collaborate with cross-functional teams to enhance operational efficiency through automation while ensuring alignment with organizational goals and objectives.



Required Skills

Proficiency in process automation using industry standard tools, with experience in configuring workflows and integrations.
Strong technical skills, including knowledge of APIs, scripting, and system integrations.
Problem-solving, debugging, and troubleshooting skills to ensure robust and operational automation systems.
Experience in project evaluation, requirements gathering, and recommending automation improvements.
Exceptional communication and teamwork skills for collaborative work with cross-functional teams.
Ability to prioritize tasks and manage multiple projects effectively.
