Internship Description

Are you a Playwright Automation Tester passionate about delivering high-quality software? We're looking for a motivated professional to join our team at I-Ray IT Solutions AB. In this role, you'll be responsible for developing and maintaining automated tests while working closely with cross-functional teams to ensure reliable software delivery.
At I-Ray IT Solutions AB, you will have the opportunity to:
• Develop and maintain automated test scripts using Playwright.
• Execute functional, regression, and end-to-end testing.
• Perform API testing and validate application functionality.
• Work closely with developers and business teams in an Agile environment.
• Identify, track, and verify software defects.
• Contribute to improving test automation processes and quality standards
With us, you can experience:
• Personal and professional growth through continuous learning.
• Opportunities to work on modern technologies and automation projects.
• A collaborative and supportive work environment.
• Flexible working arrangements where suitable.



Required Skills

What is needed in this role:
• Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field.
• Experience in test automation using Playwright.
• Knowledge of TypeScript or JavaScript.
• Experience with API testing and defect tracking tools.
• Understanding of software testing principles and Agile methodologies.
• Good analytical, problem-solving, and communication skills.
• Ability to work independently and as part of a team.
Preferred qualifications:
• Experience with Selenium or other automation tools.
• Basic knowledge of CI/CD and version control tools.
• Experience with SQL or API testing is an advantage.
