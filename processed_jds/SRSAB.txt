About Us﻿

We are passionate about research and researchers. We provide an open and curated research funding database, so that researchers can find relevant funding information faster, and have more time for research.

Our business is growing rapidly and we are looking for enthusiastic team players who have the potential to help us grow even more and possibly become a permanent team member.
