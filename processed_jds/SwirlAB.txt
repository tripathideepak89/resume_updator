About Us﻿

Swirly your wines is made and daily operated from Stockholm, Sweden 🇸🇪 by three founders Jade, Emil & Marcus who are passionate about wines, food, design & technology.

Our headquarters are in Stockholm, Sweden, where we combine our love for wine with our professional skills. Jade is a talented home cook with over 10 years of experience in mobile app development and growth, while Emil, a sommelier with a background in hospitality, electrical engineer with a master’s in robotics, and Marcus, Sommelier with world class experience, brings a unique expertise to Swirl. Together, we created Swirl to help you find wines that truly match your taste. From our personalised recommendation system to our carefully crafted features, Swirl is designed to make wine discovery easy and enjoyable.
