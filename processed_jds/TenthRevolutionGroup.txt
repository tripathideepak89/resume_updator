About the job
Senior Azure DevOps Engineer

Malmö  | Hybrid



A growing international SaaS business is looking for a Senior Azure DevOps Engineer to join its expanding engineering team in Malmö.



The company has built a successful cloud-based product used by organisations across multiple markets and is entering an exciting phase of technical growth. As the platform continues to scale, the engineering team is investing heavily in cloud infrastructure, automation, platform reliability, and Kubernetes adoption.



This is a hands-on role for an experienced engineer who enjoys taking ownership, solving complex operational challenges, and working closely with software development teams to deliver reliable, scalable cloud solutions.



The Opportunity

You'll play a key role in the evolution of the company's Azure platform, helping to improve infrastructure, deployment processes, observability, automation, and operational excellence.

The environment is collaborative and fast-moving, offering the opportunity to influence technical decisions and contribute across multiple areas rather than being confined to a narrowly defined DevOps function.



This position would suit someone who enjoys balancing day-to-day operational responsibilities with longer-term platform and automation initiatives.



What You'll Be Doing

Designing, building, and maintaining CI/CD pipelines
Managing and improving Azure cloud infrastructure
Developing and maintaining Infrastructure as Code using Terraform
Supporting and expanding Kubernetes workloads within AKS
Enhancing monitoring, reliability, and incident response processes
Collaborating closely with software engineers to improve deployment and operational practices
Driving automation initiatives across infrastructure and platform operations
Troubleshooting and resolving infrastructure and production issues
Contributing to cloud governance, security, and platform best practices
Taking ownership of projects from design through implementation and ongoing support


What We're Looking For

5+ years of experience within DevOps, Platform Engineering, Cloud Operations, or similar environments
Strong hands-on Microsoft Azure experience
Proven experience building and maintaining CI/CD pipelines
Experience with GitHub and GitHub Actions
Strong Infrastructure as Code expertise using Terraform
Experience working with Kubernetes and containerised environments
Hands-on experience with AKS
Strong operational mindset with experience supporting production environments
Excellent troubleshooting and problem-solving skills
Ability to work independently and take ownership of technical initiatives
Strong communication skills and fluent English


Why Join?

Join a growing product-focused technology business with ambitious plans
Work in a highly collaborative engineering culture
Take ownership of meaningful infrastructure and platform initiatives
Influence the direction of cloud and DevOps practices
Gain exposure to modern cloud-native technologies and automation projects
Hybrid working environment in Kraków


If you're looking for a role where you can make a genuine impact, work across a broad technical landscape, and help shape the future of a growing cloud platform, we'd love to hear from you.
