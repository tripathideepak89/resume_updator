About Us﻿

Homer is an app designed to help home owners manage any and all information about their home: everything from floorplans to receipts to model numbers and user manuals. Helping home owners keep track of this information digitally represents a lot of actual value: both in time and money.

Homer's mission is to maximise the benefit of owning a home, and minimise the friction of being a home owner.
