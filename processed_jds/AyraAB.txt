About Us﻿

Join Ayra, a fast-moving AI startup based at the Stockholm School of Economics Business Lab – the launchpad where companies like Voi and Klarna began.

We automate organic social media content marketing and help organisations connect their content directly to sales. As an intern, you’ll work side-by-side with the two founders and two founding employees, getting a front-row seat to how a modern AI startup is built.



Field﻿

IT



Internship Description

In this role you will:
• Work hands-on in our full-stack codebase and internal tools
• Help build and improve our AI-powered content and sales automation platform
• Ship features end-to-end: from idea, to prototype, to production
• Collaborate on experiments with real customers and see your work in the wild
• Influence product decisions and suggest new ideas and features

This is a huge learning opportunity for someone who is proactive, curious, and genuinely excited about AI and product engineering. We’re a small team, so you’ll get real responsibility from day one – and we expect you to take ownership, drive your own tasks, and come with solutions, not just questions.

For the right person, there is a long-term opportunity to grow into a key role and eventually take over running a significant part of our product offering.



Required Skills

Required Skills
• Strong interest in AI, software engineering and product development
• Solid programming skills in TypeScript
• Basic understanding of web development / full-stack concepts (APIs, databases, front-end vs back-end)
• Comfortable working in a startup environment: fast pace, ambiguity, and changing priorities
• Highly proactive and independent – you can take a vague problem, break it down, and execute
• Strong communication skills in English, both written and spoken, Swedish also Ok.
• Bonus: experience with any of the following – React, Node.js, cloud platforms, LLMs, prompt engineering, or social media / growth experiments

You don’t need to tick every box, but you do need to be hungry to learn, build, and ship.
